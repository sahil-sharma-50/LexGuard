# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Hackathon judges, technical reviewers, and curious visitors who need to understand the product and inspect truthful evidence without credentials.
- A paper-trading operator who uses existing private server/CLI workflows outside the public browser experience. The public console is not an operator surface for a real account.

## Product Purpose

Lexguard makes an autonomous options decision understandable and governable. It turns point-in-time market evidence, a bounded AI catalyst argument, deterministic risk certification, and paper-broker activity into a replayable case file. Success means a visitor can understand why the agent acted or abstained and safely exercise a synthetic browser-local simulation without weakening or reaching the real fail-closed broker boundary.

## Positioning

The AI argues from supplied evidence but cannot select contracts, size, price, or broker actions. Deterministic code alone issues the risk certificate and controls the paper execution boundary. Abstention and refusal are first-class successful outcomes rather than missing data.

## Operating Context

- Public evaluation begins on a scroll-driven landing page and continues into redacted case files, research status, system health, and a public console view.
- The public console provides a browser-local deterministic simulation of supervised actions. Real paper operations remain outside the anonymous browser experience.
- The agent evaluates SPY, QQQ, and IWM at fixed intraday windows and uses Alpaca evidence, a calibrated forecast, deterministic candidate generation, an AI catalyst classification, and broker-truth reconciliation.
- Archived replay must remain useful when markets, credentials, or providers are unavailable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The public site exposes sanitized, read-only evidence and must never render credentials, private account identifiers, or private broker exports.
- The command center's operator controls are real but **stop-only**: pause, resume, emergency stop, and per-case veto, gated by an operator token that lives only in session memory. They write ledger artifacts the scheduler honors on its next tick; no HTTP route can submit a broker order, and a human can never initiate a trade.
- The Training Room (`/console/**`) remains a synthetic, browser-local, confirmation-gated simulation that never calls Alpaca or mutates external state.
- The system never uses real capital. Unknown broker state, stale or future evidence, invalid AI output, missing risk state, or unavailable live data cannot authorize entry.
- Every trade candidate is a covered, equal-ratio, same-expiration four-leg structure. The model cannot choose symbols, strikes, quantities, prices, or broker actions.
- Performance, research, deployment, and competition claims appear only when backed by verified artifacts; otherwise the UI names the state honestly.
- Accessibility, keyboard operation, responsive layouts, reduced-motion behavior, and legible status communication are release requirements.

## Brand Commitments

- Product name: Lexguard.
- Core line: “AI argues. Risk decides.”
- Product language uses case, evidence, argument, verdict, certificate, order lifecycle, reconciliation, and broker truth precisely.
- The experience must feel authored, credible, and polished rather than like a generic AI-generated SaaS template.
- The public landing page and operational console use separate routes and distinct interaction modes while sharing one identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- A deterministic archived abstention case and responsive replay UI.
- Redacted FastAPI projections and resumable server-sent events.
- Tested risk, execution, reconciliation, migration, and reporting code.
- Submission copy, demo script, cover image, and claim-to-evidence map under `docs/submission/`.
- No verified OOS research result, paper-forward performance series, competition account evidence, production URL, or final submission proof exists yet. Future work must not fabricate these items.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence precedes interpretation.
2. Risk authority remains deterministic and inspectable.
3. Refusal is a valid, visible decision.
4. Public transparency stops at the privacy boundary.
5. Public demo actions are isolated, deterministic, reversible through reset, and fully audited in that browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All primary workflows must remain understandable without color alone, operable by keyboard, readable at mobile and desktop widths, and usable with reduced motion. Status, uncertainty, and unavailable evidence must be expressed in text rather than implied visually.
